# Playbook: question set for a guest, interview, or meeting

Use when the owner asks for questions to agree with a guest, an interviewee, or meeting participants before the session.

## Input

- Who the session is with and why (topic, format, length).
- Source material the owner supplies: book, article, earlier transcript, agenda, the guest's own notes.
- Anything the owner wants to avoid or must not ask.

If the topic or length is missing, ask one short question. Otherwise draft from what is available and list the gaps.

## Output

1. One line: the angle of the session.
2. Opening question that is easy to answer and sets the theme.
3. Eight to twelve main questions in a logical arc, each one line. Mark the two or three you would not drop with ★.
4. Two follow-up questions that test a claim in the source material, each with the page, timestamp, or passage it rests on.
5. One closing question.
6. "Check before sending": facts in the questions that you could not confirm from the supplied material.

Finnish by default. Write questions the guest can answer in speech: one idea per question, no stacked sub-questions.

## Quality bar

A junior producer could send this to the guest after a light edit. Every factual premise in a question is either sourced or flagged.

## Never

- Invent quotes, figures, or events in the guest's life.
- Include private memory, other guests' material, or anything the owner marked off-limits.
- Send the questions anywhere. The owner shares them.
