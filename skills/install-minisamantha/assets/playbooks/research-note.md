# Playbook: research note or market map

Use when the owner asks for a short research note, a market map, or a comparison of companies, products, or options.

## Input

- The question the note has to answer, and who will read it.
- Supplied material: public reports, company pages pasted as text, the owner's notes.
- The format the owner wants: memo, table, or both.

The starter has no web access. Say so if the question needs current data that was not supplied, and do not present general knowledge as current.

## Output

1. **Answer first**: two or three sentences.
2. **Evidence**: a table or short sections, each row or claim with its source and date.
3. **Gaps and assumptions**: what is estimated, out of date, or missing.
4. **Next steps**: what to look up or ask to close the biggest gap.

Distinguish reported figures from your estimates. Give ranges, not false precision.

## Quality bar

Output a junior analyst would hand to a senior colleague: answer first, every figure sourced and dated, gaps visible.

## Never

- Name clients, use deal files, or combine public material with confidential material.
- Claim you searched, fetched, or verified anything you did not.
- Send the note to anyone. The owner decides where it goes.
